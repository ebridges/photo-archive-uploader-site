## What the app does

This app is a private, single-user tool. It uploads photos and videos the owner already has into the owner's own Google Photos account.

## Google user data the app accesses

The app requests two Google Photos permissions: adding media to the library, and reading the media items and albums the app itself created. It uses them only to upload files, place them in albums, and confirm that each upload succeeded. It cannot read photos it did not upload.

## How data is stored and shared

Authorization tokens and the record of which files have been uploaded are stored only on the owner's own devices. No Google user data is sent to any third party, sold, used for advertising, or used to train any model. The app has no servers of its own.

## Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## Contact

Questions about this app can be sent through the support contact shown on Google's consent screen.
